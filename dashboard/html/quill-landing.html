Names

It mishears “Yusdrayl” once. Then never again.

Invented names come back mangled — it is the one thing transcription is reliably bad at. Write the fix once and Quill rewrites every transcript and recap that carried the wrong spelling, then keeps the mistake as an alias so the old links still resolve.

Consent

Nothing is recorded without asking

Quill asks every player before it keeps a second of audio. Consent is per person and per campaign, and anyone who declines is never recorded — not in session one, not in session forty.

Withdraw and Quill drops that voice from the recordings it holds and from the transcripts already written. The recaps stay. The audio does not.
