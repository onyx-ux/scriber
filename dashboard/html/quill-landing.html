Names 02

It mishears “Yusdrayl” once. Then never again.

Invented names come back mangled. Write the fix once and every transcript and recap reads it the right way, past sessions included.

Before a fix applies, Quill tells you how many lines it will change. Taking a fix back restores every one of them.

Who is recorded 03

Nobody is recorded by accident

Quill only ever records people on a campaign's roster. Anyone else in the voice channel is skipped, not recorded and thrown away.

How somebody gets onto the roster is decided once, by whoever starts the campaign.

Players choose

Each player is asked in a DM, can say no, and can stop later with /campaign consent.

The table has agreed

The DM adds people directly. Quill messages nobody, so telling the table is the DM's to do.

Not on the rosterNever recorded
Stopping laterFrom that moment on
Sessions already writtenStay as they are
Where the audio goesSaid before you agree

What was already written up is the whole table's record of an evening everyone played, so stopping does not reach back into it.
